About us

A corner of Kaluahi Chowk for friends and family

We opened Mr. Cafeteria in April 2023 with one idea: good pizza should not cost a fortune in Madhubani. My brother and I sat at the chowk for weeks, watching where people gathered in the evenings, before we put up the first pink signboard.

Today the board glows at night and the kulhad chai is always on. Families come for pizza, college friends for rolls and milkshakes, and many come back just for a ₹10 cup of chai. We still taste every new sauce ourselves.

  • Veg and non-veg pizzas, clearly separated on the menu
  • Small and medium sizes, so you pay for what you eat
  • Pizza, Chinese, rolls, momos and shakes under one roof
  • Kulhad chai in earthen cups, from ₹10
  • Combo meals with rice, starters and fries from ₹209
